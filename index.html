Hermes Google Access

A private personal automation tool, used by one person only.

What this app is

Hermes Google Access is a personal, single-user tool built and used by Jan Dolezal to let his own self-hosted assistant work with files and messages in his own Google account. There is no service, no sign-up and no other users. It is not distributed and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads and writes Google Drive files and folders that belong to the account owner (university coursework, thesis documents, spreadsheets).
  • Creates and edits Google Docs and Google Sheets in that same account.
  • Reads and creates events in that same account's Google Calendar.
  • Searches and reads messages in that same account's Gmail, read-only. It cannot send, change or delete mail.

Whose data

Only the data of the account owner, who authorises the app on his own account. Nothing is collected from anyone else, and no Google user data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. See the privacy policy for details.
